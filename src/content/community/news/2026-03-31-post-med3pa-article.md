Congratulations to [Olivier Lefebvre](/team/olivier-lefebvre) and
[Martin Vallières](/team/martin-vallieres) for the publication of their paper entitled
"Predictive performance precision analysis in medicine: identification of low-confidence predictions
at patient and profile levels (MED3pa I)" in the
_Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association (JAMIA)_.

The article can be found at:
[https://doi.org/10.1093/jamia/ocag034](https://doi.org/10.1093/jamia/ocag034)
