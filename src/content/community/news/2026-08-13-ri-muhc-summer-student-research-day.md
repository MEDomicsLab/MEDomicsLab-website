On August 13, 2026, members of our lab presented their research posters during the morning session at the [RI-MUHC 2026 Summer Student Research Day](https://event.fourwaves.com/2026institutesummer) held at the Montreal General Hospital.

### Presented Posters

- **An Interface for Confidence-Aware Predictive Model Evaluation: Integrating MED3pa into MEDomicsLab** _(Poster 1-05)_
  Presented by Thanh-Tri Nguyen (co-authors: Olivier Lefebvre, Martin Vallières). This work embeds the model-agnostic MED3pa predictive confidence framework into the no-code MEDomicsLab platform, enabling clinicians and researchers to interactively visualize subgroup reliability and patient-level confidence scores without programming.

- **Multimodal Artificial Intelligence for Clinical Diagnosis and Prognosis: Reconstructing the INSPECT Framework for Pulmonary Embolism Evaluation** _(Poster 2-14)_
  Presented by Steven Ravary (co-authors: Hakima Laribi, Martin Vallières). This project establishes a localized multimodal validation pipeline at the RI-MUHC by reconstructing the INSPECT benchmark (longitudinal MEDS-formatted EHRs and volumetric CTPA embeddings).
