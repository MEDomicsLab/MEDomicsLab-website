Congratulations to [Mahdi Loutfi](/team/mahdi-loutfi) and
[Martin Vallières](/team/martin-vallieres), as well as their co-authors,
for the publication of their paper entitled
"Optimizing renal mass histology prediction with artificial intelligence using handcrafted radiomics"
in the _World Journal of Urology_.

The study uses CT radiomics and machine learning to distinguish clear cell renal cell carcinoma from other renal mass histologies. The best model achieved an AUC of 0.88 and a specificity of 93% on a hold-out set.

The article can be found at:
[https://doi.org/10.1007/s00345-026-06296-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00345-026-06296-2)
