Congratulations to [Mariem Kallel](/team/mariem-kallel) for having successfully completed her
thesis defense and her MSc in Software Engineering at Université de Sherbrooke.

During her time at MEDomicsLab, Mariem contributed to the development and evaluation of
[MEDomics](https://medomics.app/), the lab's open-source platform for medical data modeling. Her
work focused on improving medical AI workflows and making the platform more accessible to
clinicians and interdisciplinary research teams.

We wish her success in her future projects.
