[Steven Ravary](/team/steven-ravary) and Simon Provost attended Mila's Community of Practice on Digital Health. The event brought together researchers, healthcare professionals and AI practitioners to discuss health-data governance, access, methods, applications and value creation, with two expert panels, a presentation by Daniela Witten (University of Washington), and a networking cocktail.
