Congratulations to [Nicolas Raymond](/team/nicolas-raymond) and
[Martin Vallières](/team/martin-vallieres) for the publication of their paper entitled
"Machine learning strategies to predict late adverse effects in childhood acute lymphoblastic leukemia survivors"
on arXiv. Currently under revision by _Communications Medicine_, the authors also deposited their work on
arXiv to accelerate the sharing of their research results in precision oncology with the scientific community.

The article can be found at: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.13188](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.13188)
