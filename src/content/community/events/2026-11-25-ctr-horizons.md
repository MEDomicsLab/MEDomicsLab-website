[Mahdi Loutfi](/team/mahdi-loutfi) and [Juan Duran](/team/juan-duran) will give student presentations at **CTR Horizons 2026**, and [Martin Vallières](/team/martin-vallieres) will take part as an invited panelist. The conference takes place on November 25 and 26, 2026 at the Holiday Inn & Suites Montreal Centre-ville Ouest.

Organized for the fourth consecutive year by the McGill Centre for Translational Research in Cancer (MCTRC), CTR Horizons brings together innovators, investigators, clinicians, trainees and industry professionals working in cancer translational research. It aims to encourage exchange across sectors, strengthen collaborations, and accelerate the translation of discoveries into clinical applications.

## Lab contributions

**Panel 5: Multimodal Data & Missing Modalities** (Thursday, November 26, 3:30 PM to 5:05 PM), chaired by Farhood Farahnak (Medical Physics Unit, McGill Department of Oncology):

- **Martin Vallières**: _MEDomics: A Collaborative Research Platform for Medical Data Modeling_ ([MEDomics platform](/research/medomics-platform))
- **Mahdi Loutfi** (trainee presentation): _MEDical Image Analysis with Machine Learning (MEDiml): Strategies for Simpler Radiomics_ ([MEDiml](/research/mediml))

The panel also features Morteza Rezanejad (Concordia University) on AI-powered digital pathology and John Kildea (McGill Department of Oncology) on the future of patient-centered data platforms.

**Juan Duran** will present his doctoral work on [multimodal fusion of histopathology and clinical data for survival prediction in oncology](/research/multimodal-survival-prediction).

## Program overview

- **Wednesday, November 25**: opening remarks, panels on RNA technologies and on therapeutics and omics, followed by a poster evaluation and networking cocktail
- **Thursday, November 26**: patient voice, an industry spotlight, panels on bringing discoveries to the clinic, liquid biopsy and novel diagnostics, and multimodal data, followed by the award ceremony and closing remarks
