[Ouael Nedjem Eddine Sahbi](/team/ouael-nedjem-eddine-sahbi) took part in the **Digital Health Solutions Co-creation Workshop 2026** (_Atelier de co-création de solutions numériques en santé_), held on November 12 and 13, 2026 at the Agora du Mila in Montréal.

This two-day workshop was run by the Réseau santé numérique (RSN) with the Pôle de santé publique, the Centre de recherches mathématiques (CRM), Mila, Université de Montréal and the Research Institute of the McGill University Health Centre (RI-MUHC), with support from the Fonds de recherche du Québec (FRQ).

Teams of students from health, data and AI, design and the social sciences worked with patient and citizen partners on a real digital health question, taken from a situation a patient had lived through. The first day had a panel, thematic tables and guided workshops. The second day had a feasibility session and ended with a deliverables fair, where each team presented a roadmap, a mock-up and a visual written in plain language.
