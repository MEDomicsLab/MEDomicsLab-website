[Martin Vallières](/team/martin-vallieres) and [Mahdi Loutfi](/team/mahdi-loutfi) took part in the **13th RRCancer & Marathon of Hope Québec (MOH-Q) Symposium**, held from September 30 to October 2, 2026 at the Amphithéâtre Pierre-Péladeau of the CHUM in Montréal. Mahdi Loutfi gave an oral presentation on [MEDiml](/research/mediml), the lab's open-source Python package for medical image analysis and radiomics with machine learning, and Martin Vallières was a member of the organizing committee.

This biennial symposium is hosted by the Quebec cancer research network (RRCancer), supported by Oncopole and the Quebec Breast Cancer Foundation, in partnership with the Marathon of Hope Québec. It opened with a day for student members on September 30, followed by two days open to all RRCancer members, in a hybrid format.

## Themes

- Preclinical models, translational and clinical research
- Molecular mechanisms
- Targeted therapies and immunotherapies
- MOH-Q and multiomics
- Learning equitable system
- Microenvironment and environmental factors

## Program highlights

- Keynote by Michael J. Mitchell (University of Pennsylvania) on lipid nanoparticles for RNA therapeutic delivery
- Talks by RRCancer regular members, and oral and video presentations by new researchers, including Mahdi Loutfi's presentation on [MEDiml](/research/mediml)
- A career panel organized by the RRCancer student committee
- Participation of patient partners throughout the program
