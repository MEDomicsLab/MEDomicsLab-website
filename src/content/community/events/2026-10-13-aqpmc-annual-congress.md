[Mahdi Loutfi](/team/mahdi-loutfi) and [Juan Duran](/team/juan-duran) gave oral presentations online at the **2026 Annual Scientific Congress of the AQPMC**, and [Martin Vallières](/team/martin-vallieres) was an invited speaker at the in-person day in Québec City.

The AQPMC (Association québécoise des physiciens médicaux cliniques) represents medical physicists working in Quebec hospitals. Its 2026 annual congress was held in a hybrid format: virtual presentations from October 13 to 22, 2026, followed by an in-person day of presentations and workshops on Saturday, October 24, 2026 at the CIC de Québec.
